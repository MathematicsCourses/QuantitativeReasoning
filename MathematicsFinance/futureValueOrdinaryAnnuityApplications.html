(5.) Deborah's goal is to create a college fund for her son.
She found a fund that offers an APR of 5%.

$ r = 5\% = \dfrac{5}{100} = 0.05 \\[5ex] Monthly\;\;deposits \rightarrow Compounded\:\:monthly \rightarrow m = 12 \\[3ex] FV = \$88000 \\[3ex] t = 12\;years \\[3ex] PMT = \dfrac{r * FV}{m * \left[\left(1 + \dfrac{r}{m}\right)^{mt} - 1\right]} \\[9ex] = \dfrac{0.05 * 88000}{12 * \left[\left(1 + \dfrac{0.05}{12}\right)^{12 * 12} - 1\right]} \\[9ex] = \dfrac{4400}{12 * \left[(1 + 0.0041666667)^{144} - 1\right]} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{4400}{12 * \left[(1.0041666667)^{144} - 1\right]} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{4400}{12 * (1.819848874 - 1)} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{4400}{12 * 0.8198488741} \\[5ex] = \dfrac{4400}{9.838186489} \\[5ex] = 447.2368973 \\[3ex] \approx \$447.24 \\[3ex] $ Ceteris paribus, Rita should deposit about $447.24 in the fund to accumulate $88,000 in 12 years.
